Why do bad habits feel automatic? 🧠

Every habit runs on a loop: a cue triggers a routine, and the routine delivers a reward. Repeat it enough and the brain bundles the whole sequence into a single automatic "chunk" stored in the basal ganglia — so it fires before you consciously decide. The trick isn't willpower: keep the same cue and reward, but deliberately swap the routine in the middle for one you actually want.
