Did you really decide — or did your brain decide first? 🧠

Brain-imaging studies show a burst of activity called the readiness potential ramps up before you're consciously aware you've chosen — in some setups, seconds before. Your sense of "I decided now" may be the story your mind tells after the machinery has already moved. It doesn't erase responsibility — but it does mean a "spontaneous" choice is partly an afterthought. Use it: design your environment so the automatic choice is the good one.
