What if your memories aren't recordings? 🧠

Every time you remember something, your brain pulls the memory out of storage — and for a brief window, it becomes editable. New details and your current mood quietly get written in before it re-saves. Research suggests this is why eyewitness accounts change over time, and why deliberately recalling a painful memory alongside a calmer thought can soften it.
