Why does one bad comment ruin your whole day? 🧠

Your brain isn't fair: it weighs bad more heavily than good. This "negativity bias" means a single criticism can outweigh several compliments, because threats once meant survival and your brain still treats them as urgent. The antidote is simple — when something good happens, deliberately pause for about thirty seconds and let yourself feel it, so the good one actually sticks.
