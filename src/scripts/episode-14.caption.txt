Why do some of the most testosterone-fueled people become the most generous leaders? 🧠

Testosterone is not the "aggression hormone" — it is the status hormone. It doesn't make you violent; it amplifies your drive to hold onto rank, and then boosts whatever behavior actually earns respect in your environment. In a culture where aggression wins, it fuels aggression. In a culture where generosity and fairness win, it fuels those instead. The lever you control is the room you choose to be in.
