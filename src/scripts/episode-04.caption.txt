Why do you snap before you can stop yourself? 🧠

It's not a lack of self-control — it's a shortcut your brain built for survival. A tiny structure called the amygdala can trigger a stress response before your thinking brain even gets the signal. Research shows that simply naming the emotion out loud — "I'm angry" — measurably quiets that alarm. Give it ninety seconds and it passes on its own.
