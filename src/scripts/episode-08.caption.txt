Is oxytocin really the "love hormone"? 🧠

It's more like a "tribe hormone." Oxytocin strengthens bonds and trust — but only within your group. Research shows the same release that makes you feel closer to your in-group can also make you more suspicious or indifferent toward outsiders. The good news: you can deliberately widen your circle by finding shared ground with people who feel like "them."
