Why can't you stop scrolling — even when the feed isn't any good? 🧠

Your brain runs on dopamine, and dopamine doesn't spike for the reward itself — it spikes for the maybe. A feed that pays off unpredictably (the same schedule that makes slot machines addictive) keeps that chemical anticipation running, so one more swipe always feels worth it. The fix isn't willpower — it's friction: make the app one deliberate step harder to open, and the loop loses its grip.
