Why does being left out or turned down feel physically painful? 🧠

Your brain has one pain alarm — and it fires for a broken bone the same way it fires for social rejection. The anterior cingulate cortex, your brain's danger detector, cannot tell the difference between a stubbed toe and a text that says "we need to talk." This is why isolation makes things worse: withdrawing is your brain's threat response, and it keeps you hurting longer. The antidote is also physical: social connection literally dials down the same pain circuit.
