Why does the one at the bottom burn out first? 🧠

It's not the workload — it's the rank. In baboon troops and in people, the individuals with the lowest status and the least control over their day carry the most stress hormones, and their health pays the bill. The encouraging part: research suggests that clawing back a sense of control, and leaning on people you trust, helps switch that stress response back off.

Sources:
🔗 Sapolsky — Cortisol & social rank in wild baboons (PubMed): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1287688/
