Why does long-term stress make you forgetful — and make stress even harder to stop? 🧠

Chronic stress floods your brain with cortisol, which is toxic to the hippocampus — the region that handles memory and learning. As the hippocampus shrinks, it loses its ability to shut the stress response off, creating a vicious cycle: more stress, more cortisol, more damage. The good news is that regular exercise releases BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor), which can reverse the damage and regrow hippocampal tissue.
