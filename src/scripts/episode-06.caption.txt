Why were you so reckless as a teenager? 🧠

Your brain isn't built all at once. The reward system — the "gas pedal" that craves thrills — comes online early, but the prefrontal cortex, the "brakes" that weigh consequences, keeps wiring into your mid-twenties. That mismatch is why teens feel rewards intensely while impulse control lags behind. It's not a flaw — it's a sequence. And a rested brain brakes far better than a tired one, at any age.
