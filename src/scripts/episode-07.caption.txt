Did you inherit bad genes? 🧠

Your genes don't decide your fate — they set a range of possibilities, and your environment decides where in that range you land. Some people carry gene variants that make them extra sensitive to both stress and support (the "orchid" type); others are more like dandelions — resilient in almost any conditions. Research suggests the same gene variant that raises risk in a harsh environment can also produce the best outcomes in a nurturing one.
